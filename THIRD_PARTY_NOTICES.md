### TAGNT component notices recorded in the individual files

The inspected headers describe these components; these are source attributions,
not claims that this project acquired or independently reviewed each component:

- Greek text combines readings from major editions. The primary text follows
  NA27 vocabulary, uses NA28 spelling when available, and bases punctuation and
  associated capitalization/accents on THGNT. Other readings include Scrivener
  1894 (TR), Byzantine/RP 2005, SBL/Holmes 2010, Tregelles 1879/Jongkind 2009,
  Westcott and Hort 1881, and Tyndale House GNT 2017.
- English contextual translation is based on the Berean Study Bible, with
  permission as of 1 July 2019, adapted by the source project.
- Spanish translation and contextual submeanings cite the Marvel Bible Project
  data from [OpenGNT](https://github.com/eliranwong/OpenGNT), as of 9 January 2019.
- Grammar is based on work by James Tauber, with additional details and changes
  by Tyndale scholars. The upstream header spells the name "James Tuaber".
- Dictionary forms and glosses cite TBESG, the Tyndale Brief Lexicon of Extended
  Strongs for Greek. Proper-name identification cites TIPNR.

### TAHOT component notices recorded in the individual files

- Hebrew is derived from Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC) 4.20 via
  OpenScriptures, checked against the electronic Stuttgartensia text, with
  corrections toward the Leningrad Codex by BHS or Tyndale scholars.
- Morphology comes from ETCBC; Tyndale scholars supply ketiv analyses. Codes use
  the OpenScriptures form with STEPBible adaptations.
- The source distinguishes Leningrad (`L`), qere (`Q`), ketiv (`K`), restored
  parallel text (`R`), and additional Hebrew reconstructed from LXX apparatus
  readings (`X`), as well as other manuscript or editorial variants.
- Expanded lexical tags provide disambiguated Strong identifiers, lexical forms,
  and glosses, including individual names distinguished by TIPNR references.

### Modifications made here

Fixtures select representative records from the pinned files; their provenance
is recorded in `test/fixtures/stepbible`. Deliberately malformed test cases are
identified separately from authentic source records. Conversion selects a
documented reading profile, separates source fields into the package models,
preserves source identifiers and original Unicode, and serializes deterministic
chapter JSON. Display spacing is reconstructed. No claim of an exact published
edition reproduction is made. Generated attribution records must travel with
the generated dataset.

Full original corpora and generated corpora are excluded from version control
and package publication. The byte hashes and source paths for reproducibility
are in [tool/sources.lock.json](tool/sources.lock.json).

This document records the notices found in the inspected source; it does not
claim a legal review or independently resolve the licensing of every upstream
component. Users redistributing other source fields or editions should also
inspect the preserved upstream notices for their intended use.
